FancyMenu · UI rework concept · menu bar

A menu bar that knows where you are

The bar stays at the top of every screen and of the layout editor, but now looks and behaves like a macOS menu bar: a translucent strip, rounded highlights, and menus that drop straight down from their title. The first menu, in bold, is the main one, and the middle of the bar names the screen or layout you're working on.

States

Menu titles
At rest, hovered and open. An open title keeps its highlight for as long as its menu is showing.
Unsaved changes
Save turns blue with a dot when there's something to save, and Ctrl+S still works. After saving it settles to a quiet Saved.
Collapsed
Collapsing leaves a small tab hanging from the top edge, with the FancyMenu mark so players know what it is. Click it to bring the bar back.
Light theme
Same layout, light tokens. The material stays translucent so the screen still shows through.
Solid material
With blur turned off in FancyMenu's UI settings, the bar uses the solid window color.
Narrow windows
When there isn't room, the center title steps aside first. Menu titles are never cut off.

Behavior

  • Click a title to open its menu. While a menu is open, moving to another title switches menus without another click.
  • The arrow keys move between menus and rows, Esc closes, and typing searches the menu under the pointer. Long menus show their search field right away, like in the context menus.
  • Menus are always as wide as their longest label, and nothing in the bar or its menus is ever truncated.
  • The bar follows FancyMenu's UI scale, theme and blur setting, like every other window.
