Context menus that show their state

The right-click menus are where most of FancyMenu gets configured, so they get the same controls as the windows: switches, segmented controls and values you can read at a glance. Every menu can be searched by typing, long menus get sections and show their search field right away, the most-used actions move to the top, and placeholder input works in every row that supports it.

Search
Every menu and submenu can be searched: just start typing. The text goes to the menu under the pointer, otherwise to the last menu you searched in, otherwise the main menu. Short menus keep the field hidden until you type or press Ctrl+F. Matches are highlighted, section headers stay with their matches, and the menu keeps its width while you type.
